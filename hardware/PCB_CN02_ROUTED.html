ROUTING — WHAT GOES WHERE
  • LoRa bundle (8 nets) — IO5/14/26/12/13/18/19/23 run as a parallel bundle from the right pad row into U3. No crossings.
  • I2C daisy-chain — IO21/IO22 into U4 (MPU-6050), then out of U4’s far side and down the right edge into U5 (MAX30102). Chain, not star. IO35 carries the MPU interrupt. R5/R6 are DNF: the GY-521 and MAX30102 breakouts carry their own pull-ups — fit R5/R6 (wire tails to SDA/SCL, top side) only with bare sensors.
  • GPS pair — IO16/IO17 straight across to U2, ≈54 mm clear of U3.
  • Switch group (4 nets) — IO33/32/25/27 down the centre channel into CN5; switch commons return through the GND pour.
  • Battery divider — R2/R3 hang directly off the VBAT trunk beside U7; the high-impedance tap reaches IO34 through wire link LK1.
  • Wire links — LK1 (VBAT sense), LK2 (3V3 to R1 pull-up bank), LK3/LK4 (CN4 flash UART to the module’s TX0/RX0). Plain insulated hookup wire on top.
FOR THE PERSON ASSEMBLING
  • Single-sided: every solid trace is bottom copper, drawn as seen from the component side — mirror horizontally before toner-transfer or single-layer CNC. Dashed runs are insulated wire links soldered on top.
  • GND is the pour: the whole remaining bottom copper is flooded ground. Pads marked with corner spokes tie into the pour with thermal reliefs.
  • Widths: power chain 2.0 mm, 3V3 distribution 1.2 mm, signals 0.8 mm, clearance 0.5 mm. Drills: 1.0 mm headers, 3.2 mm mounting.
  • Modules sit on female headers (dashed U1 outline = ESP32 module over its two pad rows), so traces may run beneath them — except U3/U2 LoRa, which solders flat; nothing routes under it or inside the antenna keep-out.
Three-stage power: LiPo → TP4056 → MT3608 → 5V → LM1117 → 3V3 · charge via U7 onboard USB · fit the 433 MHz antenna before first power-up.
