CN-01 — Collar Node
One full-size 830-point breadboard. ESP32 straddles the centre channel; sensors sit to the right, GPS and indicators above.
Power from USB only. The devkit's regulator feeds the 3V3 rail. Do not put the LiPo, solar panel or TP4056 on the breadboard. Fit the 433 MHz antenna before powering up — transmitting without one can destroy the SX1278.

Two resistors matter here. The DS18B20 needs a 4.7k pull-up from its yellow data line to the + rail or it reads −127. The I2C bus does not need one — the GY-521 and MAX30102 breakouts already carry their own, so leave R5/R6 for the PCB.

BS-01 — Base Station
Second breadboard. No GPS and no sensors — radio, display and four status LEDs.
Corrected pins. HARDWARE_DESIGN.md section 2.3 puts the yellow WiFi LED on GPIO5, which is already the LoRa chip-select. The firmware uses GPIO15 for yellow and GPIO13 for red. The layout below follows the firmware.

Leave the LM2596 and the 12 V adapter off the breadboard. They exist to run the finished enclosure from mains. USB is enough on the bench, and both boards must share the same radio settings: 433 MHz, SF7, 125 kHz.
