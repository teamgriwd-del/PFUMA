ROUTING — WHAT GOES WHERE
  • SPI bundle (6 nets) — IO5/14/26/18/19/23 straight across to U2 as a parallel bundle. No crossings.
  • LED group (4 nets) — IO2/4/15/13 drop left to the 330R row, then the LED row below; cathodes return through the pour. Yellow = IO15, red = IO13 (firmware pins, not HARDWARE_DESIGN.md 2.3).
  • I2C pair — IO21/IO22 out of the left row, past the R5/R6 pull-ups, into the OLED. SCL loops under the OLED pad row to keep the layer crossing-free.
  • UART — TX0/RX0 from the bottom of the right row to CN2. Flash header; hold EN/IO0 by hand or flash the module before fitting.
  • Power trunk — 12V → U4 → 5V → U5 → 3V3 along the top edge at 2.0 mm, then one 1.2 mm 3V3 branch feeds pads, pull-ups and OLED. No wire links needed on this board.
FOR THE PERSON ASSEMBLING
  • Single-sided: every solid trace is bottom copper, drawn as seen from the component side — mirror horizontally before toner-transfer or single-layer CNC. Dashed runs are insulated wire links soldered on top.
  • GND is the pour: the whole remaining bottom copper is flooded ground. Pads marked with corner spokes tie into the pour with thermal reliefs.
  • Widths: power chain 2.0 mm, 3V3 distribution 1.2 mm, signals 0.8 mm, clearance 0.5 mm. Drills: 1.0 mm headers, 3.2 mm mounting.
  • Modules sit on female headers (dashed U1 outline = ESP32 module over its two pad rows), so traces may run beneath them — except U3/U2 LoRa, which solders flat; nothing routes under it or inside the antenna keep-out.
One power chain: 12V → LM2596 → 5V → LM1117 → 3V3 · build this board first — it proves the process before CN-02 spends copper.
