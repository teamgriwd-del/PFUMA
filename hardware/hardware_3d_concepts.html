What CN-01 and BS-01 actually look like, roughly.

Isometric-style views of the populated board and the finished enclosure for both boards, built from the real pin-level design and BOM — not a stock render. For manufacturable geometry, open the .pdsprj files in Proteus; this is the "what am I building" picture that comes before that.

CN-01

Collar Node

Worn on the animal. Solar + LiPo powered, all sensors on one board, radios to the base station over LoRa.

80×50×25mm enclosure IP54 dust/splash ~1–2km LoRa range

Populated board

isometric
Layout notes: LoRa module sits at the board edge with its antenna clearing the enclosure; GPS faces skyward on the opposite side, kept ~20mm from the LoRa module per §1.4. TP4056 sits on the far side from the ESP32 to manage charging heat. Solar panel is case-mounted above the board, not on the PCB itself.

Enclosure concept

isometric
Casing notes: rounded 3D-printed pod, solar panel recessed into the top face, DS18B20 probe exits through a rear grommet to sit against the animal's skin under the strap, two 30mm strap slots on the underside, status LED visible on the front face.
BS-01

Base Station

Fixed at the farmhouse. Receives LoRa telemetry from every paired collar, forwards it to the Flask API over WiFi.

120×80×40mm project box 12V DC mains-powered ~30 collars/station

Populated board

isometric
Layout notes: no battery/solar chain — runs off a 12V adapter through an LM2596 buck converter. OLED gives local status without needing a phone nearby. Four LEDs (PWR/LoRa/WiFi/Alert) on dedicated GPIOs — IO2 / IO4 / IO15 / IO27 — with IO5 reserved solely for the LoRa chip-select, fixed from an earlier doc conflict.

Enclosure concept

isometric
Casing notes: ABS project box, OLED window and 4-LED strip on the front face, SMA antenna through the top, 12V barrel jack on the rear panel. Mounts near the router, indoors or under eaves — not rated for direct weather exposure the way CN-01 is.

Reading this honestly

These are hand-built isometric illustrations generated from the real component list and placement rules in HARDWARE_DESIGN.md and IOT_HARDWARE_GUIDE.md — component sizes and positions are representative, not measured PCB coordinates, and the enclosure renders are a styling concept, not a dimensioned CAD model ready to send to a printer.

For the real thing: the .pdsprj files in hardware/ are genuine Proteus projects — Proteus 8's 3D Visualization mode will render the actual populated board once the schematic is captured there. For the enclosure, this concept is a starting brief for whoever does the STEP/STL model, not a replacement for one.
